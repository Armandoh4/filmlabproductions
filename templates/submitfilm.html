Submit Films For Screening!

Welcome to our inaugural film festival held in London!

Submit Film! Coming Soon!

We will be hosting an awards event in a later published venue, focusing on giving a spotlight to emerging talent and smaller production companies. We are one ourselves, and have kept a collaborative mindset ever since FilmLab Productions were founded in 2022. Having won awards for our in house projects at other festivals, we now want to offer the same recognition to others. Alongside the awards we offer, we also want to share our network of hard working talent. This is something we extend to all of our participants. You do not have to be London based, but we would love to see you at our event in April.

As we filmmakers know, getting attention for our passion projects can at times feel quite soul crushing. All this on top of the effort and sacrifice that goes into turning your vision into reality. This festival is created with that experience in mind. We want to give every filmmaker a space where their work is valued, seen, and embraced by a supportive community. Our aim is to celebrate the stories that might otherwise be overlooked and to bring together people who care deeply about the craft.

We are truly excited to discover your work and to be part of your creative journey.
